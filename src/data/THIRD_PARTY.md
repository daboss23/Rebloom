# Third-party data

## canonical-face.json

The canonical 3D face from Google's MediaPipe Face Geometry module
(`mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj`), reduced to its 468 vertex positions (`v`) and
898 triangles (`f`, zero-based). Its vertices are the same 468 points the Face Landmarker finds on a photo, which is
what lets the scan's light grid follow each person's own face (DECISIONS D-077).
